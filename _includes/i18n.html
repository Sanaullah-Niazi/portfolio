{%- comment -%} Picks the language of the current page and sets: lang (en/ur/de/zh), L (link prefix: "" or "/de"), S (fixed text, from strings.yml), me (links and contact details, same in every language) and D (translated content, falls back to English). Usage: {% include i18n.html %} at the top of a page or layout; includes called afterwards can use these. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign lang = page.lang | default: 'en' -%} {%- if lang == 'en' -%} {%- assign D = site.data -%}{%- assign L = '' -%}{%- assign S = site.data.strings -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign D = site.data[lang] -%}{%- assign L = '/' | append: lang -%}{%- assign S = D.strings -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign me = site.data.profile -%} {%- assign P = D.profile | default: site.data.profile -%}
