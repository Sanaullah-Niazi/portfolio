### First Pakistani Astronaut Reaches Tiangong Space Station

Pakistan achieved a historic milestone as its first astronaut, Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, reached China's Tiangong space station aboard the Shenzhou-24 mission. Federal ministers and the Senate Chairman congratulated the nation on the successful joint space initiative with China.

### PM Shehbaz Orders Overhaul of Cybercrime Agency

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif approved the restructuring and modernisation of the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA). A federal committee was established to review reform proposals designed to improve cybercrime prevention and resolve pending cases swiftly.

<span class="brief-src">Sources: [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2046984/pm-approves-nccia-restructuring-orders-overhaul-of-cybercrime-prevention-system), [Associated Press of Pakistan](https://www.app.com.pk/national/pm-directs-swift-restructuring-modernisation-of-nccia-early-resolution-of-cybercrime-cases/)</span>

### Pakistan and China Partner on Energy Storage Projects

Pakistan and China agreed to pursue government-to-government arrangements for Chinese investments in petrol and liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) storage facilities. The partnership aims to fast-track energy infrastructure projects and improve fuel security in Pakistan.

<span class="brief-src">Sources: [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2046979/pakistan-china-agree-on-government-to-government-arrangements-for-fuel-storage-terminal-facilities), [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40450673/pakistan-amp-china-agree-to-fast-track-lpg-mogas-projects)</span>

### Pakistan Urges Human Control Over AI in Nuclear Decisions

Speaking at the United Nations, Pakistan advocated for keeping decisions on nuclear weapons usage under strict human control. Pakistani representatives warned that artificial intelligence must not be used in nuclear command and communications systems.

<span class="brief-src">Source: [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2046970/pakistan-calls-for-human-control-over-decisions-on-nuclear-weapons-use)</span>

### Maldives Airline to Launch Direct Flights to Pakistan

Maldivian, the national airline of the Maldives, announced plans to start direct flights to Pakistan to enhance bilateral trade and tourism. Furthermore, Pakistan invited investments from the Maldives into its emerging tuna-processing industry.
