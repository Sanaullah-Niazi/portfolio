### Balochistan Enacts 16 New Security Laws

Balochistan Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti announced that the provincial administration has enacted 16 new laws to strengthen law and order and enhance security infrastructure across the region. The legislative updates were outlined during a official meeting in Quetta convened to review ongoing provincial security measures.

<span class="brief-src">Source: [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2047366/16-laws-enacted-to-strengthen-law-and-order-balochistan-cm-bugti)</span>

### PDMA Balochistan Showcases Initiatives at COP31

The Provincial Disaster Management Authority of Balochistan highlighted its climate resilience strategies at the 31st UNFCCC Conference of the Parties (COP31) held in Türkiye. The participation aims to represent the province's proactive measures against climate vulnerabilities on an international platform.

<span class="brief-src">Source: [Associated Press of Pakistan](https://www.app.com.pk/balochistan/pdma-balochistan-showcases-climate-resilience-initiatives-at-unfccc-cop31-in-turkiye/)</span>

### Pakistan Marks Milestone Space Mission

Wing Commander Muhammad Zeeshan Ali arrived at China's Tiangong space station on November 25, 2026, marking a historical milestone for Pakistan's space travel achievements. The mission has prompted nationwide celebrations among citizens and students, spotlighting national progress in aeronautics.

### Pakistan Pursues Zero-Tariff Access to Chinese Market

Federal Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal held talks with senior Chinese Communist Party officials to request preferential, zero-tariff market access for Pakistani goods. Islamabad aims to scale up its annual export volume to China from approximately $3 billion to between $20 billion and $25 billion.

<span class="brief-src">Sources: [Associated Press of Pakistan](https://www.app.com.pk/national/ahsan-seeks-zero-tariff-access-to-chinese-market-to-boost-exports-to-us-25bln/), [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40451018/pakistan-seeks-zero-tariff-access-for-goods-in-talks-with-china)</span>

### Islamabad Hosts Makkah Defence Alliance Summit

Islamabad is hosting a two-day meeting of the tripartite Makkah Defence Alliance's top decision-making body. The high-level conference brings together defense officials to discuss key regional security matters, including the ongoing conflict in Yemen.
