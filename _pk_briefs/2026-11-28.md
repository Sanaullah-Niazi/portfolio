### Balochistan Security Laws and Youth Engagement

Balochistan Chief Minister Sarfaraz Bugti announced that the provincial government has enacted 16 laws to improve law and order. During an address in Turbat, he also encouraged students to reject extremism and participate in constructive political and intellectual activities.

### Teacher Appointments in Gwadar

A total of 99 schoolteachers in Gwadar have officially received their job appointment letters following a merit-based recruitment process.

<span class="brief-src">Source: [The Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2642733/gwadar-teachers-receive-job-letters)</span>

### Expansion of SME and Youth Loan Schemes

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif instructed commercial banks to ease credit requirements for small enterprises by using digital records and cash flows. Concurrently, the State Bank of Pakistan expanded the Prime Minister’s Youth Business and Agriculture Loan Scheme, raising the maximum loan limit to 25 million rupees.

<span class="brief-src">Sources: [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40451094/smes-banks-ordered-to-ease-lending-requirements), [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2047548/limit-for-youth-agriculture-loans-raised-to-rs25m)</span>

### JUI-F Protests and CDA Action

Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl reported that dozens of its workers were arrested during demonstrations across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, and Punjab. Separately, the Capital Development Authority sealed party head Maulana Fazlur Rehman's Islamabad residence after the legal property owner's identity card was cancelled.

<span class="brief-src">Sources: [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2047642/jui-f-claims-dozens-of-workers-arrested-amid-protests-across-kp-balochistan-and-punjab), [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2047652/cda-seals-fazls-islamabad-residence-after-foreign-owners-cnic-cancelled)</span>

### Severe Air Pollution in Lahore

Lahore was ranked as the world's most polluted major city after its Air Quality Index reached 400. Hazardous air pollution levels above 400 were also recorded in Peshawar and Sargodha.

<span class="brief-src">Source: [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2047659/lahore-ranked-worlds-most-polluted-major-city-sargodha-peshawar-also-record-hazardous-air)</span>
