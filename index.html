02About

How I got here

  1. Diploma, Health, Safety & EnvironmentRisk, hazards and prevention
  2. BS Information TechnologyFirst security and networking courses
  3. MS Artificial IntelligenceCGPA 3.66 · 3 papers · thesis
  4. AI × CybersecurityWhere I'm heading next

I grew up in Chaman, Balochistan. My path has not been a straight line. After school I completed a three-year diploma in Health, Safety & Environmental Engineering, which taught me to think in terms of risk, hazards and prevention. I now apply the same way of thinking to digital systems.

I then studied Information Technology at BUITEMS in Quetta, where I took my first security and networking courses. My bachelor's grades don't show my best work: I spent much of that time on cricket, football and freelance work. Towards the end of my BS I learned to manage my time and take my studies seriously.

My MS in Artificial Intelligence at The Islamia University of Bahawalpur was the turning point. I graduated with a CGPA of 3.66, published three peer-reviewed papers with different teams, and wrote a thesis on detecting AI-generated text. At the same time I taught undergraduate IT courses as a visiting lecturer.

My university offered no cybersecurity track, so I built those skills myself through Cisco's Junior Cybersecurity Analyst and Ethical Hacker programmes and a NAVTTC course in digital forensics. The motivation is also personal: I have been the victim of an online scam and of a ransomware attack, and I see how weak cyber protection still is in Pakistan. AI is making attacks faster and more convincing, and I want to work on the side that defends against them.
