## Problem
Buildings waste water when tanks overflow or run dry, and residents rarely know how much water they use or how clean it is.

## Approach
A team of three, supervised by Dr. Raja Asif Wagan at BUITEMS, Quetta. Arduino sensors measure the tank level and water quality, the pump switches on and off automatically, and an Android app shows the readings and the bill.
